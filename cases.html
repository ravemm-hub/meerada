Before and after

Real businesses picked from the middle of Google's results. We ask the AI engines the questions their customers ask, several times, and record every answer. Then we work on the business from the outside — truthful pages, listings, mentions — and ask again. Each square is one run: green = the business was named, light green = only its website was cited, grey = absent.

Method in brief: the same wording every time, no brand in the question; runs are repeated because the engines are volatile; the business is marked “named” only when a program finds it among the options in the answer text. Controls are similar businesses we do not touch. Nothing on this page can be bought; businesses were not consulted and did not pay. Raw data: visibility_state.json.
