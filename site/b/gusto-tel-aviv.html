Gusto

Italian restaurant · Tel Aviv, Israel · www.gusto.co.il
Facts checked 2026-11-25 · sources below · all profiles

Gusto is an Italian restaurant and pizzeria in central Tel Aviv, at Frishman 90 on the south-west corner of Rabin Square. The menu covers antipasti, salads, pasta, filled pasta, gnocchi, pizza, a few meat and fish mains, wine, beer and cocktails; gnocchi and pasta dishes are available gluten-free. The restaurant states it is not kosher and offers take-away, delivery and event bookings.
