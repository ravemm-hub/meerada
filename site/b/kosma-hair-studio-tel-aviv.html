Kosma Hair Studio TLV is a private one-chair hair studio at Davidka St 9 in the Yad Eliyahu neighbourhood of Tel Aviv, set in a garden. It offers custom haircuts with scalp and hair analysis, Korean (K-Beauty) hair care and Japanese-style head spa treatments, and hair-care consultations. Appointments are booked online through the studio's SimplyBook.me booking page, by phone or WhatsApp.
