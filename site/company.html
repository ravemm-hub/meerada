Why one company, not five tools

The pieces feed each other. That is the design, not an accident.

The exchange feeds everything

The Manager's ⚡ Auto picks the top-graded model for your keys from the live exchange. Guard's "burning" threshold is the exchange's measured cost for that kind of task. Cross-check's jurors are chosen from labs the exchange knows. Tribes' characters run on models the exchange ranks.

Everything feeds the exchange back

Every Cross-check verdict is stored (in the user's tenant) and becomes training data for the Judge. Every Guard alert is a data point on how models fail in the wild. Every Handshake is a measured migration. The moat is not code — it is measurement history, neutrality and continuity.

Neutral by construction

We sell no tokens and take no lab's money for a grade. A model is never judged by its own family. Sponsored grading buys measurement, not results. The battery and the checkers are public; anyone can rerun them.

Honest by default

Small samples show wide intervals. A split jury says "unknown" instead of pretending. A thin panel is labelled as such. Grades that cannot be reproduced are not grades, so we do not publish them.

"Everything under the base model will be free. What holds value is what accumulates above it: measurement history, neutrality, a reliability ledger of every model, and the tools people work through every day."
