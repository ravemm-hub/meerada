Three moves. One handshake.

01 · RECORD

Metadata in, content never out

Point meerada record at your LLM traffic. Salted fingerprints, token counts, timings, verification signals — request and response content never leaves your environment.

02 · GRADE

CPAT — cost per accepted task

meerada report ranks models on your workload: all-attempt cost over verified successes, waste breakdown, Wilson confidence intervals. Not a benchmark — your production truth.

03 · HANDSHAKEBETA SOON

Migrate without the leap of faith

Golden cases extracted from your verified history, replayed on the candidate, a per-cluster gap report, then shadow → 5% → 25% → 100% with automatic rollback. Your users never notice.

Benchmarks score answers. We score accepted work.

A model that looks cheap per token but burns reasoning tokens and needs three retries is the expensive one. Every public leaderboard measures single attempts, per token. Nobody measures the transactional unit you actually buy: a verified, accepted task at its fully-loaded cost — including the failures on the way. That is the number meerada puts in front of you, per model, per task type, with confidence intervals.
