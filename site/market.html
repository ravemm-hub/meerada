What am I looking at? Not a stock price — a live measurement. The moving number is the Meerada Grade: how well each model does real, verifiable work for what it costs. We continuously re-run a battery of checkable tasks (extraction, code, JSON, classification) against every model, verify each output programmatically, and score three axes — quality (did the task actually succeed), speed (time to an accepted result), and value (cost per accepted task). The grade rises when a model gets better or cheaper relative to its peers and falls when it slips or a rival improves. Each fresh test batch that lands nudges the number — that's the movement you see. In this demo the movement is simulated; once the canary loop is connected every tick is a real measurement with its confidence interval.
