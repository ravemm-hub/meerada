Live-style ranking of every model on every metric — click any column to sort. Prices & pro-subs are real; grades are illustrative until the live canary runs.
Columns: Grade = Meerada Grade (quality × speed × value, illustrative sample). CPAT = cost per accepted task (est). In/Out = API price per 1M tokens (real). Pro = consumer monthly subscription of that model's chat product (real where public). Usage = OpenRouter weekly token share as a real popularity proxy (blank where unavailable — never guessed). Conf = grade confidence (confirmed / provisional).
Honesty: a grade is never a bare number — API prices and pro-subs are sourced facts; grade & CPAT are sample values shown to demonstrate the board and switch to live measured numbers the moment the canary loop runs.
