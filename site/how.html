3Grey numbers are derived (estimated from list price); green and red are measured. Only measured rows can rank.
4Filter by task type or budget, then compare two models side by side — the gap report tells you what you'd save and where quality breaks.

🎛️ LLManager one app for every model, on your own keys

1Import your history: Claude Code, Claude.ai or ChatGPT export → drag the file in. Every conversation lands in one library.
2Open any conversation and keep talking — the whole history is sent along, so the new model knows everything the old one knew.
3Switch models mid-conversation (Handshake). Pick by the exchange's grade and CPAT, not by brand.
4Run many models on many tasks in parallel and keep the answer that passed. Your keys, your machine, no subscription.

🛡 Guard the watchdog for AI at work

1Guard watches your AI sessions (Claude Code, agents, batch jobs) and learns what "working" looks like: tokens per minute, files touched, progress.
2A task that stalls — same output looping, no files changing, tokens still burning — is flagged in seconds, not at the end of the bill.
3You get one alert with the numbers: what it cost so far, what it would cost if left alone, and a stop button.
4Stuck vs. working, told apart. The $10-in-one-task session that started Guard never happens again.
