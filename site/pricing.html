The battery

Questions people ask

Do you resell model access?

No. LLManager runs on your API keys; every model call is billed by the provider to your account. Meerada itself costs nothing.

Really free? What's the catch?

No catch. We are in open beta and want real users and real feedback. There is no card to enter and nothing that expires.

Will it stay free?

The exchange stays free forever. If there is real demand for the app later, paid options may come — and we will say so on this page well in advance. Nothing you use today is taken away silently.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Imports, attachments and sessions live on your machine; model calls go straight from you to the provider.

Can a lab influence its grade?

No. Everything on the exchange is measured by the same programmatic battery and published as-is.

Who is behind Meerada?

Rave Maymon (Israel) — an independent builder. The engine is open-source on GitHub.
