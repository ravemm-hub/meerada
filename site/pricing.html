Questions people ask

Do you resell model access?

No. LLManager runs on your API keys; every model call is billed by the provider to your account. You pay Meerada only for the software.

Is Pro a subscription?

One payment for 12 months of Pro. It does not auto-renew unless you choose the renewing option at checkout; the app keeps working on the free tier afterwards.

How is the license delivered?

A license key by email right after purchase. Paste it under 💎 Premium in the app — up to 3 activations (desktop, laptop, spare).

Refunds?

14 days, no questions, for Pro. Reports and sponsored grading are refunded in full if we cannot deliver.

Can a paid lab influence its grade?

No. Sponsored grading buys measurement, not results. Everything on the exchange is measured by the same programmatic battery and published as-is.

Who is behind Meerada?

Rave Maymon (Israel) — an independent builder. The engine is open-source on GitHub.
