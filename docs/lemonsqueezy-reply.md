**4. What we sell, how it's made, who it's for**

Meerada is a software product built and operated by me (Rave Maymon, Israel). It has two parts:

- **The Exchange (free):** a website that measures AI language models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, DeepSeek, Mistral, etc.) every hour on a fixed battery of programmatically verifiable tasks, and publishes each model's grade and its measured cost per verified task. It is free, funded by the paid tools below, and does not sell anything.
- **LLManager (the paid product):** a desktop application (Windows, macOS, Linux; built in Python, distributed as an installer from our GitHub releases) that lets one person work with many AI models in one place **using their own API keys** — importing their existing conversations from Claude/ChatGPT, switching models mid-conversation, running several models in parallel and comparing answers. We do **not** resell model access or tokens; every model call is billed by the provider directly to the customer's own account. Meerada charges only for the software.
  - *Licensing:* Lemon Squeezy license keys. The free tier works without a key; a **Pro** key ($99, valid 12 months, up to 3 device activations) unlocks the premium features. Validation uses your License API.
  - *Customers:* individual developers and power users who already pay for two or more AI services. Sold worldwide, online only, no sales calls.
  - *Purchase type:* single payment for a 12-month license (we may add an optional auto-renewing yearly plan later).
  - *Refunds:* 14 days, no questions asked.
- **Handshake Report / Sponsored grading:** B2B services (a written migration analysis for a company's AI workload; a paid measurement run for a model provider). These are single purchases and may be sold through Lemon Squeezy later; the exchange's grades are never influenced by payment.
