How the engines choose

People now ask an AI before they choose — a plumber, headphones, a CRM, a hotel, a web agency. We ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude the same questions and record, verbatim, which options they name, where the answers come from, how many they show, and whether the same question gets the same names a few days later.
