How it works

1️⃣

Send to all

In the Manager, one task goes to several models in parallel — or from the terminal: meerada crosscheck "…" --models a,b,c.

2️⃣

Seat a neutral jury

For each answer, up to three jurors are chosen from the other labs you have keys for. Kin never judges kin. Fewer keys? A thin panel of 1–2 still scores, labelled "examined by N models".

3️⃣

Read the verdict

Score, the jurors' own one-line reasons, flagged spans, agreement, a winner — or "no winner" when no panel agreed. A score chip lands on every examined session.

From the terminal

meerada crosscheck "Summarize this memo in 3 bullets" \
  --models gpt-5.6-luna,claude-sonnet-5,gemini-2.5-flash \
  --context memo.txt --budget 1 --store ~/.meerada/jury.sqlite
Every verdict stays on your machine (~/.meerada/jury.sqlite) and becomes the training set for Meerada Judge.
